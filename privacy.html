Privacy Policy

Shanghai Everest Chemicals Co., Ltd Privacy and Commercial Data Protection.

1. Information We Collect

When you request a chemical quote or submit an inquiry on www.evrchem.com, we collect your name, corporate email address, company name, telephone number, country, and chemical sourcing specifications.

2. Use of Information

Your details are used solely to prepare commercial quotations, furnish technical specifications (CoA, TDS, SDS), schedule shipping logistics, and facilitate international trade contracts.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or lease commercial buyer information to third parties. All inquiries transmitted through our website or EmailJS gateway are strictly confidential.

4. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding data privacy, contact our legal desk at info@evrchem.com.
