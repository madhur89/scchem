Trade Compliance

Uncompromised Export Documentation

Every consignment leaves Shanghai Everest with complete, verified paperwork to guarantee zero customs delays at your destination port.

Certificate of Analysis (CoA)

Generated by accredited laboratories using GC, HPLC, and Karl Fischer titration to certify purity, acidity, water content, and color.

Safety Data Sheets (SDS / GHS)

Prepared in accordance with the latest GHS Rev. 9 and EU REACH standards in 16 structured sections in English and destination languages.

Certificates of Origin & Inspection

CO, Form E, Form RCEP, and third-party pre-shipment inspection certificates (SGS, BV, CCIC) verifying container seal integrity and volume.
