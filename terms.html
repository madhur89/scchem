Terms of Sourcing & International Trade

Standard commercial terms and trade provisions for Shanghai Everest Chemicals Co., Ltd.

1. Quotations & Pricing

All chemical quotations provided by Shanghai Everest Chemicals Co., Ltd are governed by Incoterms 2020 (commonly FOB Shanghai/Ningbo/Qingdao or CIF destination port). Quotations remain valid for the period stipulated on the formal proforma invoice.

2. Quality Specifications & Inspection

Goods shipped correspond strictly to the agreed technical specifications and accompanying Certificate of Analysis (CoA). Buyer may elect third-party pre-shipment inspection (e.g. SGS, BV, CCIC) at port of origin at agreed terms.

3. Packaging & Transport Safety

All hazardous chemicals are packaged in strict accordance with the International Maritime Dangerous Goods (IMDG) Code using certified packaging (ISO Tanks, UN-rated steel/HDPE drums, or IBCs).

4. Governing Jurisdiction

Commercial contracts entered into with Shanghai Everest Chemicals Co., Ltd are governed by standard international trade law and arbitration mechanisms in Shanghai, China.
