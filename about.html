About Shanghai Everest Chemicals

Bridging high-capacity chemical manufacturing in China with world-class international distribution, rigorous quality testing, and dedicated supply chain mastery.

Company Heritage

35+ Years of Proven Industry Trust

Shanghai Everest Chemicals Co., Ltd is a global chemical sourcing and trading company specializing in industrial chemicals, solvents, acrylates, monomers, chlorinated solvents, and specialty chemical intermediates.

With strong sourcing capabilities across China and a rapidly growing international client base, we support manufacturers, distributors, and industrial buyers with reliable supply chains, competitive pricing, and consistent product quality.

Headquartered in the vibrant Songjiang business district of Shanghai, China, Everest Chemicals operates at the crossroads of maritime trade, state-of-the-art chemical parks, and advanced testing laboratories, enabling us to handle over 100,000 Metric Tons of chemicals annually.

Sustainable Future

Committed to Green Chemistry & Responsible Sourcing

As global chemical trade evolves, Shanghai Everest Chemicals proactively invests in eco-conscious products, including low-VOC coatings, waterborne resin technologies, and REACH-compliant alternatives.

Low-VOC & Waterborne Coating Materials:

Supplying environment-friendly coatings and high-performance resins that reduce emissions and comply with strict environmental norms.

Carbon-Conscious Maritime Logistics:

Consolidating shipments and optimizing sea-freight vessel routes to minimize shipping footprint and transit turnaround times.

Zero-Leakage Container Protocols:

Strict hydrostatic testing on all ISO tanks and UN-certified steel drums to protect personnel, waterways, and marine ecosystems.
